# Chess — showcase storyboard

## Creative brief

Audience: prospective players discovering the app on Google Play, jwtc.nl, or YouTube.

Deliverable: a 90-second, caption-only showcase uploaded to YouTube and reused in the Play Store listing and as a website embed. English is the working language. Sound is unnecessary; every message is conveyed visually.

Core message: **Play. Practice. Explore. Free, open-source chess with no ads.**

This storyboard is grounded in the current repository, including CONTEXT.md, README.md, navigation, and UI strings. Footage has not yet been captured or validated on a device. Before recording, confirm that each featured capability is in the release being promoted.

## Composition

- Master canvas: 1920 × 1080, landscape, 30 fps.
- Record the app in portrait at its native resolution. Keep its aspect ratio when placing it in the video.
- Left: caption panel, approximately x=120–1080. Right: app recording centered around x=1500, with about 960 px of usable height. Actual recording width depends on device aspect ratio; do not stretch it to fill half the canvas.
- Use a solid, warm charcoal background, off-white text, and one accent color drawn from the board. Avoid a busy chess-themed backdrop.
- Headline: approximately 68–80 px, two lines maximum. Supporting copy: 38–44 px, two lines maximum. Keep generous margins and test readability at phone size.
- Keep the app in the same location throughout. Use straight cuts for actions and brief dissolves for chapter changes. Captions remain still while the viewer watches the move.
- Prefer a flat recording with a subtle edge or shadow. A large decorative phone frame would make the board smaller.
- Keep essential app controls visible. For analysis details, use a brief enlarged crop within the right panel, then return to the full screen.

## Demo material to prepare

1. **Opening and closing position:** one clean Standard position with a visually clear finishing move. Record both the move and an extended still hold.
2. **Engine game:** a fresh Standard game with a short thinking time and clear piece movement.
3. **Mate-in-two puzzle:** a verified solution from the bundled collection, rehearsed before recording.
4. **Analysis game:** a short Standard PGN with an instructive mistake and an alternative continuation. Verify the engine's displayed classification before committing the shot.
5. **Online game:** a signed-in Lichess demo account and an arranged casual game. Keep account setup out of the footage.
6. **Saved games:** a small database with recognizable demo names and events, including the analysis game.
7. **Variant shots:** one Chess 960 opening and a verified King of the Hill win. Avoid positions close to a repetition or 50-move draw.
8. **Appearance shots:** identical board position with two or three legible combinations of colors and pieces.

Exact positions, puzzle IDs, and tap coordinates should be chosen during the recording rehearsal. Engine moves and online responses must come from real runs, rather than a predetermined script that might not match the app.

## Recording and editing checklist

- Use the release intended for the audience; repository features may precede the published version.
- Record portrait consistently, with a fixed device resolution, text scale, orientation, and language.
- Use a clean demo device or emulator. Hide notifications and keep the status bar consistent.
- Rehearse each scene, then capture individual clips with 2–3 seconds of extra footage at both ends.
- Capture enough time for viewers to see the starting position, action, and result. Cut waiting time without speeding up chess moves beyond comprehension.
- Keep touch indicators subtle and away from important board content where possible.
- Preserve genuine app UI and outcomes. Editorial captions belong outside the recording.
- Check the silent export at phone size. The board, headline, and highlighted app feedback must remain readable.
- Compare the final edit with the intended release and recheck each product claim.
- Confirm current YouTube and Play Store video requirements before uploading; this document is a creative storyboard, not a platform-compliance review.

Suggested YouTube title: **Chess for Android — Play, Practice & Explore | Free & No Ads**

Suggested thumbnail: one large, readable board position, the real app icon, and **“Just chess.”** Avoid a collage of small screens.
